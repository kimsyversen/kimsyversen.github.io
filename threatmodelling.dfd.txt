Threat modeling: Fig. 1, data flow diagram of the fictional claims portal

  INTERNET                         :  OUR CLOUD (inside the trust boundary)
                                   :
  [Sign-in, external provider] <---:--- identity ---> (Portal)
        ^                          :                   ^    ^
        | password                 :                   |    |
        v                          :                   |    +--- files ---> =Document store=
  [Customer, web browser] <--------:------ HTTPS ------+
                                   :

  [ ] external entity   ( ) process   = = data store   <--> data flow   : trust boundary

Flow description: the customer's browser sends the password to the external
sign-in provider. The provider tells the portal who the customer is, so the
portal never receives the password. The customer then uses the portal over
HTTPS to report damage and to upload and view documents. The portal writes
files to the document store and reads them back. The customer and the sign-in
provider are outside the trust boundary; the portal and the document store are
inside it.

  R  Portal
  I  Portal
  E  Portal
  T  Document store
  D  Document store
