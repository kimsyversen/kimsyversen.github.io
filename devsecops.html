Code

Build to the standard

03

Assurance · Evidence

Track whether security activities are complete; keep findings, owners and due dates visible.

04

Build

Package trusted inputs

04

Package from trusted inputs and keep the artifact traceable to its source and dependencies.

Assurance · Verify

Review test evidence against the agreed security requirements and follow unresolved findings to closure.

05

Test

Verify the controls

05

Check functionality, quality and security. Scan code, packages, secrets and infrastructure configuration.

BP4 · Production go / no-go

Require verified controls, tested recovery and monitoring. Close critical and high findings or get the system owner’s explicit risk acceptance.

Source note ↘
06

Release

Approve the version

06

Approve a verified, traceable software version after required checks pass.

Controlled promotion

Promote only the approved release and confirm the rollout is understood and reversible.

07

Deploy

Deliver with guardrails

07

Use hardened settings and least-privilege access; verify the rollout and support rollback.

BP5 · Handover

Transfer operations and open actions with named owners, dates and the evidence needed to continue.

Source note ↘
08

Operate

Run the service

08

Maintain reliability, patch vulnerabilities, manage access and backups, and respond to incidents.

Measure · Improve

Review risk, overdue actions, vulnerability age, tested requirements and recovery results on a set cadence.

09

Monitor

Learn from live signals

09

Track service health, performance, user experience and security events; alert on suspicious activity or change.

Closeout · Confirm

Assign closure ownership and confirm remaining access and data obligations are addressed.

10

Retire

Close the lifecycle

10

Decommission the service, revoke credentials and access, and handle data under retention requirements.

Case presentation · decision-point evidence

The governance checkpoints above condense the supplied case presentation. These short Norwegian excerpts show the source criteria for BP1-BP5. Other lifecycle guidance follows the supplied phase descriptions; the organization should set its own owners and thresholds.

BP1 · Classification

“Sikkerhetskriterier for go/no-go: Risikoeier er utpekt, og systemeier har godkjent sikkerhetsnivået. DPIA-behovet er avklart, og risikoer er dokumentert.”
Casepresentasjon.pdf, p. 3

BP2 · Concept choice

“Sikkerhetsbegrunnelsen for anbefalt konsept er dokumentert, og forventet restrisiko er innenfor det systemeier kan akseptere.”
Casepresentasjon.pdf, p. 4

BP3 · Design and development

“Trusselmodellen er gjennomført, og risikoregisteret er oppdatert. Sikkerhetskravene i backlog.”
Casepresentasjon.pdf, p. 5

BP4 · Production readiness

“Sikkerhetskravene er implementert og testet, og landing zone er gjennomgått.”
Casepresentasjon.pdf, p. 6

BP5 · Operations handover

“Forvaltning har tatt over driftsansvaret, og alle åpne tiltak har eier og frist.”
Casepresentasjon.pdf, p. 7

Use the checkpoints as prompts for evidence and ownership. They are a high-level view, not a substitute for the organization’s own policies, risk criteria or release authority.
