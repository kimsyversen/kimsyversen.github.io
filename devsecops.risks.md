# DevSecOps phase risk audit

Reviewed 24 November 2026 against both views of [devsecops.html](devsecops.html). The register contains 55 DevSecOps risks and 30 agentic risks. The register covers enterprise governance and secure development in every phase. A case can illustrate several risks when it documents a different failure for each one.

[DevSecOps risks](#devsecops) · [Agentic DevSecOps risks](#agentic-devsecops) · [Evidence and coverage checks](#evidence-and-coverage-checks)

## DevSecOps

Each risk sits at the phase where a team can address the failure. Governance covers ownership, oversight and decisions. Secure development covers code, tooling and service execution. **Page** identifies a risk represented in the HTML. **Added** identifies an additional risk. `[inference]`

**Direct** identifies a documented failure or consequence. A qualifier names the part the source establishes. **Partial** identifies a documented part of the scenario with a material gap in the causal evidence. **Complaint** attributes an allegation to a regulator. **Compromise** identifies a malicious change or access event with downstream harm still unknown. **Supervisory finding**, **Obligation**, **Field data**, **Study**, **Demonstration**, **Operational case**, **Analogue**, and **Control evidence** identify their respective source types. Scenario wording describes what can happen in a comparable setting. Check the evidence column for what happened in the cited case.
