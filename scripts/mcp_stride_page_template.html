Method

STRIDE per element
External entities are checked for spoofing and repudiation, processes for all six categories, data stores for tampering, repudiation, information disclosure and denial of service, and data flows for tampering, information disclosure and denial of service. Every one of these cells has at least one threat.
DREAD
Damage, Reproducibility, Exploitability, Affected users and Discoverability, each 1 to 10. The score is the mean of the five. High is 6 or above, Medium 4 to 5.9, Low below 4.
Evidence
Exploited: used in attacks, shown by a CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities entry or a vendor-confirmed incident. Disclosed: a public CVE, vendor advisory or researcher demo, with no confirmed attacks found. Theoretical: no matching public record found. Many records come from other MCP products and show that the failure type is real, not that this design is hit. The match between a record and a threat is inference
Evidence rule
Where a public record exists, Discoverability is raised to at least 7. Where the record is exploited, Reproducibility and Exploitability are raised to at least 7 too. The detail view shows which scores were raised.
Basis
V numbers point to quoted claims in the design page's fact check. Ids starting with ctrl- or gap- are controls and gaps on the design page.
